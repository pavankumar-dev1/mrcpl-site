ABOUT MRCPL

Local roots. Dependable infrastructure delivery.

Mahirex Constructions Private Limited is a Sindhanur-based construction company supporting railway, road, earthwork and building work packages across Raichur District.

WHO WE ARE

A capable local partner for essential works.

MRCPL works as a subcontractor, bringing together local knowledge, skilled manpower, available machinery and hands-on site coordination to support project teams.

We focus on completing agreed work on time, to the required quality and with attention to safety. Each package starts with a clear understanding of the scope, site needs and resources required.
