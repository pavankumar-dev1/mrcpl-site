Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains how Mahirex Constructions Private Limited ("MRCPL", "we") handles personal information collected through pavankumar-dev1.github.io/mrcpl-site. We handle personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

Only what you send us through the contact form or by calling or emailing us: your name, phone number, email address (optional) and the message you write about your project.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry.
  • To discuss and quote for the services you ask about.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies and does not load third-party fonts or trackers. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and operation.

Who processes your data

Contact form messages pass through a form-handling service that delivers them to us by email. Our hosting provider also processes technical logs. We share your details with others only when the law requires it or when you ask us to.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of the discussion, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent, by writing to mahirexconstructionpvtltd@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we receive. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
