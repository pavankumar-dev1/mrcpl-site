Services

Integrated construction, engineering, infrastructure and project support services for residential, commercial, industrial and infrastructure projects.

Scope, deliverables and responsibilities are agreed with the client at the start of each engagement.

Civil Construction and Turnkey Projects

Civil construction for residential, commercial, institutional and industrial developments, with practical planning, quality workmanship and coordination across project activities.

  • Residential construction: houses, villas, duplex homes, apartments and multi-storey buildings
  • Commercial construction: offices, retail spaces, showrooms and business complexes
  • Industrial and institutional buildings: factories, warehouses, educational buildings and training centres
  • Specialised buildings: function halls, convention centres, hospitality facilities and multipurpose buildings
  • Turnkey construction under a defined scope
  • Renovation and upgradation: extensions, remodelling, repairs and refurbishment
  • Civil and structural works: foundations, RCC, masonry, plastering, flooring and waterproofing
  • Finishing works: painting, tiling, doors and windows, facade finishing and ceiling works

To deliver functional, durable and well-coordinated buildings that meet the client's requirements for quality, cost and time.

Architecture, Engineering and BIM

Architectural and engineering documentation that supports building planning, visualisation, technical coordination and construction.

  • Architectural planning: space planning, floor plans and layouts
  • 2D drawings: plans, elevations, sections and working drawings
  • 3D modelling and visualisation: building models, exterior renders, interior views and walkthroughs
  • Structural engineering documentation, in coordination with qualified structural engineers
  • Building Information Modelling (BIM), where included in the project scope
  • Construction detailing: staircases, foundations, columns, beams, slabs and roof systems
  • MEP drawings: electrical, plumbing, sanitary, HVAC and services layouts
  • Quantity estimation and BOQ
  • Approval documentation, subject to applicable regulations and approvals

To improve design clarity, reduce coordination gaps and support informed decisions before and during construction.

Infrastructure and Land Development

Planning and execution support for orderly land development, from site assessment to roads, drainage and water supply.

  • Land surveying and site assessment, as applicable
  • Layout planning: plot subdivision, internal roads, open spaces and amenities
  • Road infrastructure: alignment, earthwork, subgrade, pavement, paver roads and asphalt roads
  • Underground drainage (UGD)
  • Stormwater drainage
  • Water supply systems: pipelines, storage tanks and overhead tanks
  • Landscaping and open spaces
  • DPR and cost estimation

To support orderly land development through practical planning, coordinated infrastructure and efficient use of available land.

Industrial Construction, PEB and Steel Structures

Construction and coordination for industrial buildings, steel structures, warehouses and large-span facilities, developed around function, structural design and site conditions.

  • Pre-engineered buildings (PEB): sheds, warehouses, workshops and storage facilities
  • Structural steel fabrication coordination
  • Steel erection in accordance with approved drawings and safety requirements
  • Roofing and wall cladding
  • Industrial mezzanine floors
  • Conventional steel structures
  • Foundation and civil works
  • Industrial flooring

To support safe, functional and efficient industrial facilities through coordinated engineering and construction practices.

MEP and Building Services

Planning, installation coordination and execution support for mechanical, electrical and plumbing systems.

  • Electrical systems: layouts, power distribution, switchboards and wiring
  • Lighting design and installation
  • Plumbing systems
  • Drainage and sewerage
  • HVAC systems: air-conditioning layouts, ducting and ventilation
  • Fresh air and exhaust systems
  • Water storage and pumping

To support buildings with well-coordinated, functional and maintainable building services.

Interior Design and Finishing

Interior planning, fit-out coordination and finishing for residential, commercial, office, hospitality and institutional spaces.

  • Interior space planning
  • Residential interiors
  • Commercial interiors
  • False ceiling works
  • Flooring and wall finishes
  • Modular kitchens and storage
  • Electrical and lighting coordination
  • Painting and surface finishes
  • Doors, partitions and glazing
  • Facade and decorative features

To create practical, well-finished spaces that reflect the client's functional needs and design preferences.

Renewable Energy and Specialised Works

Selected renewable energy and specialised construction requirements, depending on the project scope.

  • Rooftop solar projects
  • Solar mounting structures
  • Solar electrical integration
  • Energy-efficient building solutions
  • Architectural metal facades
  • Steel fabrication
  • Canopies and entrance features
  • Surveying and site measurement
  • Building inspection and defect documentation within the agreed scope

To integrate specialised solutions into building and infrastructure projects with attention to function, safety and long-term performance.

Project Management and Technical Consultancy

Project planning, technical documentation, estimation, coordination and site monitoring for construction and infrastructure projects.

  • Project planning and scheduling
  • Cost estimation
  • BOQ preparation
  • Quantity surveying
  • Tender and contract documentation
  • Site supervision
  • Quality control coordination
  • Contractor and labour coordination

To help clients make informed decisions, monitor performance and keep control over cost, quality and timelines.
